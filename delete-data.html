Delete my data

This permanently removes everything Prompt CRAFT saved: My Info, planner, receipts, My Files (including uploaded photos, documents and videos), mission progress, game scores and settings — on this device and, if you’re signed in inside Claude, in your Claude account.

⚠️ This can’t be undone. Download anything you want to keep from 💾 My Files first.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Prompt CRAFT.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my data (or go to Legal → 🗑️ Delete my data).
  3. Tap Delete all my data, then Yes, delete everything.

You can also clear the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. The app has no accounts and the developer holds no copy of your data. Questions: mnpaccity@gmail.com
