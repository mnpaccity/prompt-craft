Accessibility Statement

We want Prompt CRAFT to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which is the standard commonly used for ADA compliance.

What we’ve done

  • Everything works with a keyboard: Tab to move, Enter or Space to press, Esc to close windows. A “Skip to main content” link appears on the first Tab.
  • Clear focus outlines show where you are.
  • Pop-up windows are announced to screen readers and keep focus inside until closed.
  • Buttons, fields, images and icons have text labels; status messages are announced.
  • Text can be zoomed to 200% and the layout adapts to small screens.
  • Animation is reduced if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Video clips show on-screen captions with the voiceover text.
  • Information is never shown by color alone.

Known limitations

Some third-party sites the app links to, and AI-generated content, may not be fully accessible. Videos you upload are shown as you provided them, so add captions to your own videos.
