Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Prompt CRAFT (“the app”) is made by Minh Nguyen. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

What we collect

We do not run accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data. The developer does not receive the information you type into the app.

Where your information is stored

  • On your device: things you enter — My Info (name, contact details for auto-fill), planner events, receipts, saved prompts, game scores and settings — are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device.
  • Inside Claude (claude.ai or the Claude apps): if you open the app there while signed in, your data and any photos, documents or videos you upload to My Files are saved privately to your Claude account by Anthropic so they sync across your devices. AI features (Prompt Enhancer, Video Detective, Pro Missions coach, “Explain with Claude”) send the text or video frames you choose to Claude to generate a response. Anthropic’s privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Google Calendar (optional): only if you connect it inside Claude, the planner reads and writes the calendar events you choose.

Third-party services

The Android app and the website version load everything from their own site and do not contact third-party services. Only when you use Prompt CRAFT inside Claude does it also load fonts from Google Fonts and a ZIP helper from cdnjs (Cloudflare); those services receive standard technical information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Links to other sites (Claude, Google, video tools) open their own sites, with their own policies.

Children

The app is intended for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete everything the app stored at any time in Legal → 🗑️ Delete my data, or by clearing your browser’s site data. Uninstalling the Android app also removes its data from your device.

Security

Data on your device is protected by your device and browser. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may see it, so avoid storing sensitive information such as passwords, ID or bank numbers.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: mnpaccity@gmail.com.
